# 5. PRODUCT VISION

Sanchivue is a professional platform for conducting live technical interviews and collaborative coding sessions.

It should offer three connected experiences:

### A. Interviewer and recruiter workspace

Recruiters and interviewers can create interviews, configure questions, invite candidates, conduct live sessions, collaborate in code, record private notes, evaluate skills and produce reports.

### B. Candidate workspace

Candidates can validate their invitation, join a scheduled interview, check their audio/video devices, read problem statements, write and run code when execution is configured, communicate with interviewers and participate in technical discussions.

### C. Platform and organization administration

Authorized administrators can manage organizations, users, permissions, question libraries, settings, audit records and platform operations.

The application should be feature-rich, but not needlessly complicated. Prioritize a fully connected, working product over a large collection of disconnected screens.
